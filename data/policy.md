# Northstar Outfitters support policy (fictional)

Use only this policy when answering customers. Explain the next step without claiming that an action has already been taken.

## R1. Standard returns

Unused, non-final-sale items may be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery. Day 30 is eligible. Returns require an order number. Customers pay return shipping for change-of-mind returns. Original shipping charges are non-refundable on standard returns. Refunds go to the original payment method within 5–10 business days after the warehouse receives the return.

## R2. Damaged or incorrect items

Customers can report damaged or incorrect items within 45 calendar days of delivery, including final-sale items. Day 45 is eligible. Ask for the order number and a photo of the damage or incorrect item if those are missing. Support reviews the claim and provides a prepaid label if approved. Do not promise approval, a replacement, or a refund before review.

## R3. Cancellations and address changes

Customers can request a cancellation or an address change before the order ships. Ask for the order number if missing. For address changes, direct customers to the secure account portal to provide the new address. Submission does not guarantee that the warehouse can stop the order. Once the order ships, neither request can be made through Northstar.

## R4. Delivery issues

For a package marked delivered but missing, ask the customer to check safe places and with household members. If it is still missing 48 hours after the delivery scan, support can open a carrier trace using the order number. Do not promise a refund or replacement during the trace. For an in-transit package, support can open a trace after seven calendar days without a tracking update.

## R5. Exchanges and gifts

Northstar does not process direct exchanges. An eligible item can be returned and the desired item ordered separately. An eligible gift return with a gift receipt is issued as store credit. Without a gift receipt, a standard return requires the order number and refunds the original payment method.

## R6. Privacy and authority

Never ask for passwords, full payment-card numbers, or full shipping addresses in chat. If a customer already shared sensitive details, do not repeat them. Use the secure account portal for address changes and payment updates. Support can explain the process and collect order numbers, but a chat response cannot confirm that any account action occurred.
